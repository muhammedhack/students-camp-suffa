Scholarly Faculty & Mentorship

People of AL-IRTIQA-26 (علماء وهيئة الإشراف)

Guided by celebrated senior jurists, university research scholars, language trainers, and the dedicated youth vanguard of ADSA Melmuri.

Chief Patron & Keynote

Dr. C.K. Muhammed Abdurahman Al Faizy Al Azhari

Principal Mudarris, Alathurpadi Dars & Member, Samastha Consultative Council
🎓 PhD (Al-Azhar University, Cairo) • MA Islamic Jurisprudence

One of South India’s most venerable Islamic scholars and jurists, guiding hundreds of scholars who now lead institutions, universities, and scholarly councils globally. He delivers the inaugural keynote and valedictory address.

Featured Session: Keynote: "The Art of Ascension: Classical Depth & Global Responsibility"
Senior Academic Mentor

Usthad [Senior Dars Mudarris]

Faculty of Classical Hermeneutics & Usul al-Fiqh
🎓 Senior Dars Fazilat • Classical Epistemology Specialist

Renowned mentor specializing in classical Arabic jurisprudence texts, guiding students on maintaining uninterrupted spiritual discipline, awrad, and rigorous textual comprehension during collegiate life.

Featured Session: Masterclass: "Preserving Dars Discipline in the University Seminar"
Higher Education Strategist

Prof. [University Admissions Advisor]

Faculty of Humanities & University Entrance Director
🎓 PhD Comparative Literature (JNU) • Postdoc (SOAS London)

Distinguished scholar and admissions strategist who has mentored over 500 madrasa and dars graduates into Central Universities including JNU, Jamia Millia, EFLU, and Delhi University.

Featured Session: Workshop: "CUET-PG Blueprints & Central University Roadmaps"
Language Lab Lead

Dr. [Trilingual Faculty & Linguist]

Assistant Professor of Functional Arabic & Translation
🎓 MA Arabic & English Literature • Certified Diplomatic Translator

Specialist in contemporary academic and spoken Arabic, functional essay structuring, and simultaneous translation for international scholarly symposia.

Featured Session: Intensive Lab: "Functional Academic Arabic & Discourse"
Research Methodology Guide

Dr. [Research & Dialectics Guide]

Senior Fellow, Center for Dialectical & Historical Studies
🎓 MPhil, PhD Philosophy • Research Consultant

Expert in bridging classical Islamic Munazarah (dialectics) with modern research methodology, abstract writing, citation standards, and reference management.

Featured Session: Dialectics Lab: "Munazarah Logic Applied to Modern Debate"
Organizing Secretariat

Camp Coordinator (ADSA)

General Convener, Alathurpadi Dars Students’ Association
🎓 Senior Dars Scholar • Youth Leadership Coordinator

Leading the central organizing committee responsible for academic scheduling, cohort logistics, admissions registration, and participant hospitality.

Featured Session: Secretariat: "Cohort Orientation, Camp Ethics & Feedback"
Hospitality & Dining Lead

Hospitality Convener (ADSA)

In-charge, Campus Facilities & Central Dining Hall
🎓 Alathurpadi Dars Alumni Council

Supervising the seven-day traditional dining experience, dietary health protocols, hostel accommodation, and hospitality services.

Featured Session: Support Desk: "Dining Coordination & Special Dietary Care"
Volunteer Captain

Corps Leader & Mentorship Peer

Head, ADSA Student Volunteer Corps
🎓 Final Year Scholar • Community Organizer

Directing over 40 dedicated student volunteers ensuring seamless audio-visual operations, session check-ins, prayer hall organization, and campus cleanliness.

Featured Session: Logistics: "Camp Operations & 24/7 Scholar Assistance"

Lifelong Academic Fellowship

Mentorship in AL-IRTIQA-26 does not terminate with the camp closing ceremony. Each scholar is paired with a designated senior advisor who provides continuous guidance on entrance preparation, SOP drafting, and scholarship applications throughout the academic year.
