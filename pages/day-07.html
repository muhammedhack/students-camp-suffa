Day 07 // 27 Dec 2026, Sunday

Reflections, Certificates & Closing

أُفُقُ الغَدِ وَخِتَامُ المَسِيرَة — സമാപനം

The culminating valedictory chapter: finalization of individual one-year academic roadmaps, awarding of verified course completion certificates, valedictory address by Dr. C.K. Muhammed Abdurahman Al Faizy Al Azhari, and farewell prayer.

Day 07 Master Schedule

Sessions, Workshops & Keynotes

08:30 AM – 10:30 AM

Personal 1-Year Academic Action Plan Review

Individual review of application deadlines, scholarship submission dates, and target entrance examinations.

Personal Mentors & Advisors • Advising Hub
11:00 AM – 01:00 PM

Grand Valedictory Assembly & Presidential Address

Concluding keynote addressing the moral compass, scholarly perseverance, and future obligations of the cohort.

Dr. C.K. Muhammed Abdurahman Al Faizy Al Azhari • Main Irtiqa Auditorium
01:00 PM – 02:15 PM

Valedictory Royal Feast (Special Dining)

Celebratory feast featuring Royal Malabar Biryani, desserts, and affectionate farewell camaraderie.

Dining Service • Central Dining Hall
02:30 PM – 04:00 PM

Certificate Conferral & Participant Reflections

Conferral of verified AL-IRTIQA-26 completion certificates, cohort group photographs, and student testimonials.

ADSA Presidium & Guests • Main Irtiqa Auditorium
04:30 PM – 05:30 PM

Solemn Farewell Halaqa & Concluding Du’a

Traditional closing du’a, seeking divine acceptance and blessings for each student’s upcoming academic journey.

Alathurpadi Dars Mudarriseen • Central Dars Plenary Hall
