Dining Logistics

Central Dining Hall Protocols & Etiquette

Preserving classical Dars table hospitality, zero food wastage, and wholesome communal camaraderie.

Zero Food Wastage Pledge

Delegates are urged to take only what they can consume. Any surplus wholesome meals are packed and distributed to local community outreach programs in Melmuri.

Sunnah Table Manners

Beginning with Bismillah, eating with the right hand, drinking while seated in measured sips, and concluding with grateful supplications for the camp cooks and donors.

Dietary & Medical Helpdesk

Scholars with specific dietary allergies, diabetes, or medication requirements may contact the volunteer catering desk for customized bland preparations.
