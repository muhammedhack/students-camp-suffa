Inquiries & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (الأسئلة الشائعة)

Find answers to common inquiries regarding candidate eligibility, registration deadlines, daily camp routine, hostel accommodation, dining, and certificates.

General Camp Questions

AL-IRTIQA-26 (الارتقاء ٢٦) is an intensive seven-day residential higher-studies orientation camp organized by Alathurpadi Dars Students’ Association (ADSA) in collaboration with the SUFFA Dars Network. It bridges classical Islamic scholarship with contemporary university entrance requirements, providing students with admission roadmaps, language mastery, and academic research skills.

The camp is specifically curated for final-year students currently studying in SUFFA Dars institutions across Kerala who are preparing to transition into central universities, state universities, or international institutions in the upcoming academic cycle.

The camp takes place from Monday, 21 December to Sunday, 27 December 2026 at the historic Alathurpadi Dars Campus (Halaqatudduroos), Melmuri, Malappuram, Kerala.

Registration & Selection

Registration opens online on 16 November 2026 and closes on 10 December 2026. Applicants submit their academic background, target disciplines, and a recommendation from their Dars Mudarris. The selection committee evaluates applications, and the final list of admitted scholars is announced on 14 December 2026.

No. AL-IRTIQA-26 is a community-supported initiative organized under the patronage of Alathurpadi Dars and ADSA. Boarding, lodging, session materials, and wholesome meals are fully sponsored for all selected scholars.

Selected candidates will receive an official notification via WhatsApp and email on 14 December 2026 containing their candidate ID, room allocation, and pre-camp reading materials.

Schedule & Campus Life

The camp maintains the sacred discipline of the traditional Dars. Days begin with Tahajjud and Subh prayers followed by morning awrad, breakfast, academic plenary keynotes, midday prayer and lunch, afternoon interactive workshops and language labs, evening sports/tea, and nocturnal mutala’a.

Yes. Because the curriculum is progressive—each day building directly upon the previous day’s learnings—full residential attendance from 21 December 08:00 AM to 27 December 05:30 PM is strictly required.

Sessions are trilingual. Classical theological and linguistic lectures are delivered in Arabic and Malayalam, university entrance and research workshops are conducted in English, and specialized subcontinental discourse is held in Urdu.

Accommodation & Dining

Yes. All participants stay in comfortable, clean campus dormitories at Alathurpadi Dars. Wholesome traditional meals—including Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Tea, and Dinner—are prepared under strict hygiene standards in the central dining hall.

Scholars should bring personal notebooks, essential kitabs for nocturnal mutala’a, personal prayer mats, formal traditional attire (white jubbah / mundu), toiletries, any required personal medications, and a valid photo ID.

Yes. Participants with allergies or dietary needs should notify the ADSA Hospitality Desk upon arrival so customized meals or herbal remedies can be arranged.

Certification & Mentorship

Yes. Scholars who successfully complete all seven days and submit their 1-Year Academic Action Plan will be awarded a verified Certificate of Academic Orientation during the Grand Valedictory assembly.

Each scholar is paired with a designated ADSA alumni mentor currently enrolled in their target university. Mentors assist with CUET-PG application reviews, Statement of Purpose revisions, and interview rehearsals throughout the year.

Still Have Questions?

The ADSA Organizing Secretariat is ready to assist prospective scholars and parents with any specific inquiries regarding admissions, travel, or accommodations.
