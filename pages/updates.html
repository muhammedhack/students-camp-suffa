Official Noticeboard & News

Camp Updates & Bulletins (المستجدات والإعلانات)

Official notifications from the ADSA Organizing Secretariat regarding registration timelines, participant selection, keynote confirmations, and campus logistics.

Admissions 📅 16 November 2026 • 3 min read

Online Registration Commences for AL-IRTIQA-26 Higher Studies Camp

Alathurpadi Dars Students’ Association (ADSA) officially announces the opening of registrations for AL-IRTIQA-26. Final-year students from SUFFA Dars institutions across Kerala preparing for university admissions are eligible to apply through the digital demo portal.

Applicants will participate in seven residential days covering CUET-PG exam blueprints, trilingual debate laboratories, Statement of Purpose clinics, and individual mentorship pairing. Application submission requires academic background verification and recommendation from local Dars mudarris.
Keynote 📅 20 November 2026 • 2 min read

Chief Patron Dr. C.K. Muhammed Abdurahman Al Faizy Al Azhari to Deliver Inaugural Keynote

The Camp Presidium confirms that revered jurist and Samastha Consultative Council member Dr. C.K. Muhammed Abdurahman Al Faizy Al Azhari will deliver the inaugural keynote address on Monday, 21 December 2026 at 11:00 AM.

The keynote, titled "The Art of Ascension: Classical Depth & Global Responsibility", will set the scholarly and ethical horizon for the entire cohort at the Alathurpadi Dars plenary auditorium.
Deadline 📅 01 December 2026 • 2 min read

Final Reminder: Application Portal Closes on 10 December 2026

Prospective participants are advised that the deadline for submitting online registration forms and verification documents is strictly 10 December 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Late submissions will not be accommodated.

The selection committee evaluates candidate academic consistency, linguistic interest in Arabic/English/Urdu, and long-term university ambitions to assemble a diverse and committed cohort of scholars.
Results 📅 05 December 2026 • 2 min read

Selected Scholar List Scheduled for Publication on 14 December 2026

The final list of scholars admitted to AL-IRTIQA-26 will be released on 14 December 2026 through the camp noticeboard and transmitted directly to participants via verified WhatsApp notification.

Admitted candidates will receive their digital camp badge, room allocation number, and comprehensive pre-arrival preparatory reading pack.
Camp Life 📅 08 December 2026 • 3 min read

Seven-Day Food & Dining Menu Published by ADSA Hospitality Desk

In accordance with traditional Sunnah hospitality, the comprehensive 7-day culinary schedule has been published, featuring traditional Malabar welcoming feasts, Thalassery Jumu’ah Biryani, Arabian Qahwah, and wholesome nutrition.

Special dietary requests, allergies, and herbal tea requirements can be registered directly with the hospitality coordinator upon arrival at the central dining hall.
Logistics 📅 12 December 2026 • 3 min read

Reporting Protocols & Hostel Check-In Instructions for 21 December

All admitted scholars must report to the Alathurpadi Dars Campus Foyer in Melmuri, Malappuram between 08:00 AM and 10:30 AM on Monday, 21 December 2026 for room key issuance and orientation briefing.

Scholars should bring personal notebooks, essential kitabs for nocturnal mutala’a, personal prayer mats, formal traditional attire, and a valid photo identification card.

Organized by Alathurpadi Dars Students' Association (ADSA), fostering intellectual elevation, scholarly rigor, and international academic opportunities for future leaders.
