Interactive Learning Halaqas

Academic Labs, Workshops & Activities

Immersion environments bridging theoretical depth with hands-on scholarship: academic writing clinics, trilingual debates, simulated interview panels, and dialectics.

Clinic 01 // Scholarly Expression

Academic Writing & Research Labs

Moving beyond traditional rote memorization into structured academic prose. Participants learn to formulate provocative thesis statements, structure research abstracts, navigate academic journal databases (JSTOR, Brill, Shamilah), and implement standard citation manuals (APA 7th, Chicago 17th, and MLA 9th).

Abstract Drafting Zotero & Mendeley Anti-Plagiarism Standards

Clinic 02 // Eloquence & Dialectics

Trilingual Debate & Toastmasters Circles

A rigorous arena where scholars test their spontaneous oratory and dialectical poise. Conducted under British Parliamentary and Oxford debate formats in Functional Arabic, Academic English, and Urdu, students learn to defend complex propositions under timed constraints with peer evaluations.

Functional Arabic Oratory Oxford Debate Rules Classical Munazarah
Explore Day 04 Language Schedule →
Clinic 03 // Admission Simulation

Simulated University & Scholarship Panels

Demystifying the high-stakes university admission interview. Scholars sit before a rotating panel of university professors and international alumni who pose authentic viva questions, evaluate body language and vocal delivery, and provide instant diagnostic rubrics.

JNU & JMI Viva Simulation Scholarship Panel Drills Personal Pitch Refining

Holistic Development

Specialized Formats & Camp Life

Nocturnal Mutala’a Circles

Nightly self-paced study sessions in the historical Dars library, where scholars unpack complex classical texts and consult senior faculty on personal research dilemmas.

Grand Campfire of Reflections

An open-air evening assembly of heartfelt reflections, classical Arabic and Urdu poetry recitations, and intimate spiritual counsel under the Malappuram night sky.

One-on-One Mentorship Pairing

Individualized 45-minute advising slots pairing each participant with an ADSA alumni scholar currently studying in their target discipline or university.

Organized by Alathurpadi Dars Students' Association (ADSA), fostering intellectual elevation, scholarly rigor, and international academic opportunities for future leaders.
