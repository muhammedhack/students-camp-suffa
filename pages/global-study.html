5-Step Strategy

The 5-Step University Admission Roadmap

From classical Dars sanad equivalence to foreign embassy attestation and scholarship interviews.

Phase 01 // Months 01 – 03

Dars Equivalency & Degree Validation

Acquiring secondary certification (NIOS / State Open), Afzal-ul-Ulama degrees, and university recognition equivalence certificates.

Phase 02 // Months 03 – 05

Entrance Exam Preparation (CUET-PG & GRE)

Rigorous practice on previous question papers, general paper comprehension, analytical reasoning, and specialized domain syllabi.

Phase 03 // Months 05 – 07

Academic Writing & SOP Formulation

Crafting distinct, intellectually coherent Statements of Purpose, research proposals, and securing faculty recommendation letters.

Phase 04 // Months 07 – 09

International Attestation & Visa Dossiers

Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) apostille, embassy consular authentications, Arabic translation of credentials, and passport readiness.

Phase 05 // Months 09 – 12

Counseling, Admission & Matriculation

Seat acceptance, campus housing enrollment, orientation travel, and establishing peer mentorship with the global ADSA scholar network.
